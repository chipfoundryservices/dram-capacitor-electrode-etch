# Glossary: DRAM Capacitor Electrode Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**Actinometry:** Normalizing an emission line by an inert-gas line (Ar 750.4 nm) to remove changes in electron density and temperature. (Ch. 8.2)

**Al marker:** The transient Al emission as the 0.3 nm Al₂O₃ layer at mid-ZAZ etches; used to predict each wafer's ZAZ clearing time. (Ch. 8.5.4)

**ALE (atomic-layer etching):** Etching by alternating self-limiting modification and removal steps; no loading; ≈ 0.1–0.4 nm per cycle. (Ch. 4.10, 7.8, 14.2)

**Antenna ratio:** Exposed conductor area collecting plasma current divided by the dielectric area it is connected to. ≈ 0.017 for the wafer-continuous plate. (Ch. 13.3)

**Array edge:** Boundary between the capacitor array and the periphery; the plate extends 1.5 µm beyond the last dummy row. (Ch. 1.4.3)

---

## B

**BARC:** Bottom anti-reflective coating under the plate resist; 60 nm organic. (Ch. 2.7)

**BCl₃:** Boron trichloride; oxygen getter that makes TiO₂ and high-k chlorination exothermic; deposits BₓClᵧ at low ion energy. (Ch. 3.2, 4.2–4.3, 7.5)

**Bevel etch:** Removal of films from the outer 1–2 mm of the wafer to prevent flakes and arcing. (Ch. 9.6)

**Breakthrough (BT):** Short BCl₃-rich, higher-energy step that removes the TiOₓNᵧ surface oxide before the TiN main etch. (Ch. 3.2.3)

---

## C

**Cell plate:** The shared top electrode of an array block: TiN top electrode, SiGe fill, W strap. (Ch. 1.1, 2.4–2.6)

**Charging (plate):** Voltage across the capacitor dielectric from plasma non-uniformity, driven through the plate, node, and junction circuit. (Ch. 13)

**Clearing-time spread:** Variation of local clearing time from film-thickness and rate non-uniformity, √((σ_t/t)² + (σ_R/R)²). (Ch. 6.1)

**Cup:** The concave pillar top left by carrying the fill dimple down in an anisotropic etch-back; ≈ 10 nm in the reference. (Ch. 10.3)

**Cusp:** The V-shaped bottom of the fill dimple, leading into the seam; deepened by isotropic etching. (Ch. 2.1.4, 10.3.3)

**Cylinder electrode:** A TiN liner in the hole (rather than a fill); separated using a sacrificial fill. (Ch. 14.1)

---

## D

**Dimple (fill):** Depression in the TiN surface over each filled hole; depth t − √(t² − r²) = 9.8 nm for an 18 nm fill in a 32 nm hole. (Ch. 2.1.4)

**Dummy rows:** Inactive pillar rows at the array edge. (Ch. 1.4.3)

---

## E

**Edge ring:** Consumable ring around the wafer whose erosion tilts and weakens the edge ion flux. (Ch. 6.4)

**Endpoint (EP):** Detection of a film clearing from OES, reflectometry, or RF signals. (Ch. 8)

**EOT:** Equivalent oxide thickness; 0.50 nm for the reference ZAZ. (Ch. 2.3)

**Etch–deposition transition:** Ion energy below which BCl₃ plasmas deposit BₓClᵧ on oxides instead of etching; ≈ 60 eV for ZrO₂ at 60 °C. (Ch. 4.3.2)

**Etch-back (storage-node):** Blanket plasma removal of the field TiN to separate the storage nodes; the reference route of this book. (Ch. 1.2.3, 10)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving-average feedback used to set the SNS overetch time. (Ch. 15.6.1)

---

## F

**Field TiN:** The part of the TiN fill lying on the top SiN surface outside the holes; 18 nm. (Ch. 2.1.3)

**First-wafer effect:** Different etch behaviour on the first wafers after a clean or idle, from a changed wall state. (Ch. 9.1.2)

**Flare (hole-top):** Chamfer at the top of each capacitor hole from mask and nitride faceting; ≈ 3 nm deep. (Ch. 2.1.2)

**Foot notch:** Lateral notch at the bottom of the SiGe at the plate edge from the Ge-rich initial layer; 1.5–3 nm. (Ch. 12.4)

---

## G

**Grain tail:** The statistical distribution of residual ZrO₂ grains at the end of the HK step, which sets the high-k overetch. (Ch. 4.7)

---

## H

**Hard-mask route:** Plate patterning with an oxide hard mask, allowing a hot HK step; the oxide stays as ILD. (Ch. 7.3, 12.9)

**HK step:** The BCl₃/Cl₂ step of the plate etch that clears the top-electrode TiN and the ZAZ. (Ch. 4.4, 12.1)

**Hybrid removal:** Dry HK step stopped before clearing, finished with a short wet etch. (Ch. 4.8.2)

---

## I

**IEDF:** Ion energy distribution function; bimodal at 13.56 MHz; its high-energy tail sets SiN loss in the overetch. (Ch. 5.3.3)

**Island (plate):** One separate plate per bank; 32 per die, ≈ 1.1 × 1.2 mm, 5.4 × 10⁸ cells. (Ch. 2.7)

---

## L

**Loading constant (k):** Parameter in ER = ER_max/(1 + kθ_A) describing radical depletion by the exposed film; ≈ 1.2 for the SNS main etch. (Ch. 3.4)

**Loading jump:** Increase in the TiN rate when the field clears and only pillar tops remain: (1 + k)/(1 + kθ_A) = 1.76. (Ch. 3.4.2)

---

## N

**Node comb:** Electrical test structure of two interdigitated storage-node sets; measures shorts. (Ch. 15.2.3)

**Node-to-node short:** Conducting residue between two storage nodes; any continuous thread is a hard short. (Ch. 1.2.2, 10.5)

---

## O

**OCD:** Optical critical-dimension metrology (scatterometry); measures rim recess, SiN, plate-edge profile. (Ch. 15.3)

**Overetch (OE):** Etch time after endpoint; in SNS, 10 s at ≈ 50 eV; in HK, 50% of the Al-marker clearing time. (Ch. 6.1, 10.2, 12.6)

---

## P

**Periphery contact open:** Periphery contact that fails to open because ZrO₂ residue stops its fluorocarbon etch. (Ch. 1.3.3, 16.3)

**Phase A / B / C (charging):** Wafer-continuous plate; island separation; isolated islands. (Ch. 13.2)

**Pillar-top fraction:** Fraction of the array covered by pillar tops, 0.464 at 32 nm top CD. (Ch. 3.4.2)

**Post-etch treatment (PET):** Downstream plasma (H₂/N₂, H₂O) to remove surface halogens before air exposure. (Ch. 11.2)

**Pull-back (resist):** Lateral recession of the resist edge during the etch; ≈ 0.15 × vertical loss. (Ch. 12.2.2)

---

## Q

**Queue time:** Allowed time between steps; fill → SNS ≤ 24 h, plate PET → ILD ≤ 8 h. (Ch. 11.6)

---

## R

**Rim recess:** Depth of the pillar rim below the SiN surface after SNS; 7.3–9.9 nm in the reference. (Ch. 10.2)

**Rim radius:** Rounding of the pillar-top edge; ≥ 5 nm keeps the dielectric field enhancement near 1.5×. (Ch. 11.3.3)

---

## S

**Seam:** Plane where the TiN fill fronts met; etches faster than bulk (ratio f). (Ch. 2.1.3, 10.3.4)

**Seam groove:** Groove below the cup bottom from seam-preferential etching: (f − 1) × exposed etch. (Ch. 10.3.4)

**SiGe plate fill:** B-doped poly-Si₀.₇Ge₀.₃, LPCVD 425 °C, 150 nm above the array. (Ch. 2.5)

**SNS (storage-node separation):** Removal of the field TiN to isolate the storage nodes; module 1. (Ch. 1.2, 10)

**Storage node:** The private bottom electrode of each cell; a TiN pillar. (Ch. 1.1)

---

## T

**Top electrode:** 5 nm ALD TiN on the dielectric; the electrode of the plate side. (Ch. 2.4)

**TiN notch:** Lateral recess of the top-electrode TiN under the SiGe edge during the HK step; ≈ 4 nm. (Ch. 12.5)

**TXRF:** Total-reflection X-ray fluorescence; measures surface metals including Zr residue. (Ch. 15.4.2)

---

## V

**Veil:** Thin wall of redeposited Zr-containing material left standing at the plate edge after resist strip. (Ch. 4.9, 12.7)

**Voltage contrast (VC):** E-beam inspection mode that distinguishes grounded and floating conductors; detects shorts in monitor arrays. (Ch. 15.2.1)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless auto-clean):** Plasma clean of the chamber between wafers; chlorine before fluorine when Zr or Ti is on the wall. (Ch. 7.6, 9.2–9.3)

**Wall saddle:** Depression in the narrow SiN wall between nearest-neighbour hole tops, caused by the flares; 3–6 nm; sets the minimum rim recess. (Ch. 2.1.2, 10.2.3)

**W strap:** 40 nm PVD W on the SiGe plate; carries most of the plate current (3.6 Ω/□ total). (Ch. 2.6)

---

## Z

**ZAZ:** ZrO₂/Al₂O₃/ZrO₂ capacitor dielectric; 2.6/0.3/2.6 nm, EOT 0.50 nm, crystallized tetragonal after the SiGe fill. (Ch. 2.3)
